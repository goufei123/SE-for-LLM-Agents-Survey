# Suggesting Literature

To suggest an addition or correction, open an issue containing:

- Paper title and DOI, arXiv, or publisher link.
- Relevant lifecycle stage and engineering problem.
- A short explanation of how the work studies the Agent system's development, design, integration, assurance, or evolution.
- For a correction, the affected entry and supporting source.

Cross-stage contributions are welcome. Existing entries follow the current manuscript; additions will be reviewed before integration. Please link to source papers rather than uploading third-party full texts.
