# Literature Reading Guide

The five guides below reproduce the organization and representative comparisons in the lifecycle chapter of *Software Engineering for LLM-Based Agents: A Lifecycle-Oriented Survey*.

| Stage | 中文 | Discussed studies |
|---|---|---:|
| [Requirements Engineering](requirements-engineering.md) | 需求工程 | 16 |
| [Architecture and Design](architecture-and-design.md) | 架构与设计 | 39 |
| [Implementation and Integration](implementation-and-integration.md) | 实现与集成 | 45 |
| [Testing and Assurance](testing-and-assurance.md) | 测试与保障 | 32 |
| [Maintenance and Evolution](maintenance-and-evolution.md) | 维护与演化 | 32 |

There are 146 distinct studies across these guides, including main-analysis and supplementary discussions. A paper can contribute to multiple lifecycle stages and therefore appear on more than one page. This is a curated index of the studies discussed in the manuscript, not the full screening pool or a replacement for the manuscript's collection statistics.

Each guide includes:

- The topical subsections used in the manuscript.
- Engineering-problem and approach comparisons, preserving the manuscript's key ideas.
- Full study titles, bibliographic years, publication information, and external links.

Publication metadata is taken from the manuscript snapshot, rather than being presented as a fresh publication-status audit. Supporting foundational SE references are listed separately. The manuscript remains the source for detailed evidence, evaluation limits, and findings.
